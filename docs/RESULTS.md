## Interpretation limits

A and B are separate blocks; no pooling. B was selected for candidate-set differences and includes pilot-exposed episodes. Gold is incomplete judgement from one labeller who had read the show. The held-out v2 result is post-hoc. Zero discordance or a zero-width bootstrap interval does not prove equivalence.

See [P08](../logs/P08_report.md) and [P08b](../logs/P08b_report.md) for sensitivities, selection history, structural misses and full limitations.
